# Code of Conduct

Zerix follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

- Be professional and respectful.
- No harassment, discrimination, or doxxing.
- Technical criticism should target the code, not the person.
- Violations can be reported to **conduct@zerix.dev** (confidential).

## Enforcement

Reports will be reviewed by the maintainers. Possible actions include:
a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from contributing.
