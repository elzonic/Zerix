| Name | Role | Focus |
|---|---|---|

## Criteria to become a maintainer

- 10+ merged, high-quality PRs.
- Deep understanding of the Zerix threat model.
- Commitment to reviewing PRs regularly.

## Responsibilities

- Review PRs within 5 business days.
- Keep CI green.
- Respond to security reports within the SLA in `SECURITY.md`.
- Participate in roadmap discussions.
